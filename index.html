Service details

Check-up

A check-up is a visit where a dental professional listens to your concerns and checks your teeth and gums.

Not sure what to book? Message us and we’ll help you get started.

What it is

We’ll talk about what brought you in, then review your teeth and gums. Your clinician can explain what they notice and discuss possible next steps.

Who it may be for

A check-up may suit you if you’d like to discuss your oral health, have a concern, or are returning to the dentist after some time away.

What happens during the visit

1 Tell us what you’d like help with.

2 Your clinician checks your teeth and gums and listens to your concerns.

3 You’ll discuss what was found and possible next steps.
